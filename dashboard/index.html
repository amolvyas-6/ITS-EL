Mathematical Foundation

Graph Model

The ORR corridor is modeled as a weighted directed graph G = (V, E, w) where:

  • V = intersections and destination zones
  • E = directed road movements along the corridor
  • w: E → ℝ⁺ = free-flow travel time in seconds

Markov Chain

Vehicle routing follows a discrete-time Markov chain with:

  • State = current intersection occupancy
  • T[i][j] = routing probability from junction i to j
  • P(t+1) = P(t) · T over signal cycles
  • MFPT = ANTT to destination zones

ATMS Optimization

Adaptive signal timing combines local green extension with downstream coordination:

  • Self-loop relief = shorter red-phase hold at bottlenecks
  • Downstream coordination = more flow toward viable exits
  • v/c monitoring = saturation and queue-alert detection
